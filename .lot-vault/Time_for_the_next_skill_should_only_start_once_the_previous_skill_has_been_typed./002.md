Started. Read the current implementation — confirming the diagnosis and the plan.

## What happens today

Two separate timers, and one prompt-wide `hintShown` flag:

1. `store.syncHintTimer()` arms `hintDelayFor(state)` (400 + 300×score ms) and
   is re-armed after *every* state change. When it fires it sets
   `prompt.hintShown = true`.
2. `store.syncHintReveal()` then chains: one reveal unit of the caret word is
   uncovered immediately, and one more every `HINT_REVEAL_COOLDOWN_MS` (1000ms)
   — **on a chain of timers, independent of whether anything has been typed.**

That chain is the bug you're describing: sign 2 of a word starts counting down
the moment sign 1 was revealed, not when sign 1 was typed.

Scoring compounds it: `awardFinishedUnits` awards CLEAN_AWARD only when
`!p.hintShown` — a single prompt-wide boolean. So once the hint appears for
*any* sign, every remaining sign in the prompt drops to BASE_AWARD even if you
type them instantly. Hence "I should still be scoring two points".

## Plan

Make the hint per-skill-occurrence rather than per-prompt, and anchor its
countdown to typing progress:

- `Prompt.hintShown: boolean` → `Prompt.hintedUnits: Set<number>` (the
  translation-unit indexes whose hint has been revealed).
- Scoring: unit *i* is clean iff it has no mistakes **and** `i` is not in
  `hintedUnits`. Signs typed before their own hint keep +2, regardless of what
  happened earlier in the prompt.
- One timer in the store, armed for **the unit at the caret only**, and
  re-armed only when the caret moves to a different unit. So revealing sign 1
  does not start sign 2's clock — sign 2's clock starts when the caret reaches
  sign 2, i.e. when sign 1 has been typed.
- A caret sitting on a space is a unit with no skills, so no timer runs there.
  That gives the space behaviour you asked for for free: the first sign of a
  word starts its countdown only once the space before it is typed.
- Delay stays as today: `hintDelayMs(score)` for the prompt's first hint,
  `HINT_REVEAL_COOLDOWN_MS` for later signs.
- The store's `hintUnitsRevealed` / `hintRevealKey` / `hintRevealTimer`
  bookkeeping goes away — the reveal state now lives in the core (and so
  persists across a reload, which it previously didn't).
