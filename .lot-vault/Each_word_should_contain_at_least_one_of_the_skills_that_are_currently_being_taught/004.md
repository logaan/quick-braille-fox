Done — prompts now pad only with words that teach something current.

**What was wrong:** filler words were drawn from everything the learner
could read, so "bad cab" kept showing up hundreds of prompts later and
most keystrokes drilled nothing.

**What changed** (`src/core/prompts.ts`):

- Filler is restricted to words exercising a skill *currently being
  taught* — the 5-skill active window, plus the target itself when the
  prompt is a revision of an already-learnt skill.
- Capitals prompts now capitalise **every** word of the sequence rather
  than one, since no plain lowercase word exercises a capital indicator.
- The word pools translate each corpus word once and keep its skill ids,
  replacing three separate re-translation passes.

**One honest limitation.** Some windows contain nothing a plain word can
show: five digits in a row, the capital indicators, a run of punctuation.
There, no word can satisfy the rule. Rather than reopening the whole
corpus, the padding falls back to words built from the *most recently
learnt* skills, so it is at least fresh revision. Punctuation prompts are
the visible case — `bad cab.` can only ever put the mark on one word.
If you want those tightened further (e.g. one-word prompts for terminal
marks), say so and I will.

**Verified:** typecheck clean, 1642 tests pass (three new ones assert the
property across the whole curriculum, on revision, and through a
300-prompt session). Also driven in headless Chrome against the dev
server — a fresh session gave `ebb cab cab`, `dad baa ace`, `dab face
add` with a–e active, and later `hid jig fig` with f/h/j/k/l active.
