# Scoring

Here is how scoring should work:

A user has a score that it tracked per skill.
The score starts at 0.
If the user reaches a score of 10 they know that skill.
Every time they type the skill they get at least 1 point.
If they type the skill before the hint becomes visible then they get 1 additional point.
If they mistype the skill once and then correctly type the skill they get one point.
If they mistype the skill twice then they lose point.

For example if it's early on and I'm being tested on individual characters the sentence I have to type may be: "a cad ebb".
If need the hints but make no mistakes then I will score 2 points for a, 1 point for c, 1 point for d, and 2 points for b.

Points are scored immediately
