Done — scoring now works per the spec, merged to main (merge of branch `worktree-scoring-per-occurrence`, commit 7551102; worktree and branch cleaned up).

What changed:

- **Every skill occurrence scores, immediately as it is typed** — not just the prompt's target at completion. Occurrences are the prompt text's translation units, so contractions, digits, and indicator signs (capitals, number sign) all score with their unit. In "a cad ebb" with hints and no mistakes: a=2 (two occurrences), c=1, d=1, e=1, b=2 — exactly the example.
- **+2 clean / +1 otherwise**: an occurrence typed with no mistake before the hint became visible earns 2; hinted or once-mistyped-then-correct earns 1 — typing a skill always earns at least a point. Awards keep the hint state from the moment of typing: units typed before the hint appeared keep their +2 even if the hint shows later in the prompt.
- **Mistakes are per occurrence**: first mistype on an occurrence is free (drops it to +1); the second on the *same* occurrence costs 1 point immediately (floored at 0) and force-shows the hint. Mistakes on different occurrences no longer compound.
- **Reaching 10 = learnt** (was strictly greater than 10).
- qbf challenge unchanged (never scores). Saved progress still loads (serialization updated leniently).

Verified two ways:

1. Unit tests rewritten around the new model (including a test encoding the "a cad ebb" example); full suite + typecheck green.
2. Drove the real app in headless Chrome against the dev server: watched a score tick up +2 mid-prompt the instant its character was typed; completed "add bad add" hinted → a=3, d=5, b=1 (1 per occurrence); two mistakes on the same spot dropped the skill a point and forced the hint; a reload mid-prompt restored everything; a stored score of exactly 10 shows as learnt ("1 of 258 skills learnt", skill leaves the active five).

One thing to note: scores now climb noticeably faster (a 3-word prompt can be worth ~10+ points across its skills, and the target skill often appears several times in one prompt). If skills start feeling "learnt" too quickly, the knob to turn is `LEARNT_THRESHOLD` in src/core/types.ts.
