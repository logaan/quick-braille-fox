# Tutor

Grade 1 Braille (also known as uncontracted braille) is the simplest form of the tactile reading and writing system. It features a direct, letter-by-letter, and word-for-word translation of standard print—every letter, number, and punctuation mark is represented by its own individual braille character without any shorthand contractions.

Grade 2 Braille is a contracted form of the tactile alphabet used for books, menus, and signage. It uses 180 abbreviations and contractions (like shorthand) to save space, shorten document lengths, and increase reading speed.

The tutor will first teach you grade 1 Braille character by character. Then it will move on to Grade 2 braille abbreviations and contractions.

At any given time you will be learning 5 new things. They may be letters, numbers, symbols, abbreviations, or contractions. The general term for these things is skills. The tutor will assign a score to your knowledge of each skill. When your score for a skill gets above 10 the tutor considers it learnt and will introduce the next skill. Making mistakes can lower your score, even on learnt skills. At any given time only the 5 skills you're closest to learning will be taught.

Alongside the skills being taught you will also be revising items you already know. The revision items will come half at random, and half based on what you have the lowest scores for.
