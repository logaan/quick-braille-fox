# Q: how should the hint delay grow with skill score?

You specified a 400ms hint delay for a skill with 0 points, growing as the score grows, and hint-only-after-two-mistakes once a skill is learnt (score > 10).

**Assumption I'm proceeding with:** delay = 400ms + 300ms × score, so roughly 400ms at score 0 up to ~3.4s at score 10, then switching to the "only after two consecutive mistakes" rule once learnt.

Happy with that curve, or do you want different numbers / a different shape (e.g. exponential)?
